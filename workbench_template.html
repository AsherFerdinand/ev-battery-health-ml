Charge and ageing example

SOC and SOH

SOC is charge remaining divided by the capacity currently available. Capacity-based SOH is available capacity divided by fresh nominal capacity. An aged cell can be fully charged and still store less charge than a fresh one.

Capacity loss and resistance

Loss of cyclable lithium and active material reduces capacity. Increased internal resistance increases voltage sag and heating under load. These effects can vary independently.
