    Engineering consideration

    Charge and ageing example

    A separate educational illustration; it does not change the simulation above or predict ageing.

    SOH sets the usable-capacity ceiling.

    SOC is the fraction of that currently usable capacity which remains charged. Lower SOH does not make the case smaller.

    At 80% capacity SOH and 50% SOC, the simplified remaining charge is 0.8 × 0.5 = 40% of nominal capacity.

    Why batteries warm under load

    Current through internal resistance produces heat: Ploss = I²R. Heat transfer, thermal mass, cooling and operating conditions also affect temperature. The thermometer above is the existing lumped ECM thermal state, not a precise cell-temperature prediction.

    Capacity can fade as active material or cyclable lithium is lost. Resistance may also rise, but these changes depend on chemistry, use and ageing mechanism and need not follow one fixed ratio.
